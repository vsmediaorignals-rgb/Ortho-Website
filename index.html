      About the Surgeon

      Precision Meets Compassion

      Dr. Aryan Mehta is a board-certified orthopedic surgeon with over 18 years of experience in joint replacement, minimally invasive arthroscopy, and complex trauma reconstruction. He trained at AIIMS New Delhi and completed fellowships at the Royal College of Surgeons, London, specialising in robotic-assisted arthroplasty.

      His patient-first philosophy combines cutting-edge surgical techniques with evidence-based rehabilitation protocols, resulting in a 98% patient satisfaction rate and consistently shorter recovery times.
